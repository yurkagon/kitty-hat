# Game interface

Mode: Operate. Mobile-first shared-phone game. Direction is pinned by the approved implementation plan; code-led build.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A welcoming illustrated party game whose private word, timer and next action are always unmistakable.

OWN-WORLD: Cream paper ground, forest-green ink and primary actions, coral skip controls, butter-yellow accents. Gouache hat and player-owned cat portraits made from user-provided photos. Comfortaa headings, Nunito Sans body. Soft offset depth on large rounded buttons.

STORY: Name empty teams from suggestions or custom text, add players through a portrait-and-name selection modal, write private words, pass the phone, explain through three rounds, celebrate the scores.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact hat wordmark and sound control; a large centered illustrated hat; the title Капелюх and invitation; a prominent green start button followed by three concise round labels. Game screens replace the illustration with the active player, remaining time, a large word slip and plus/minus actions.

FORM: User-approved cozy board game direction. Signature interaction: a paper word slip briefly turns as the next word is drawn, with the new word already visible. No seed overrides the pinned brief.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
